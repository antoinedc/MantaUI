Mockup notes (throwaway)

How to use it

  • Switchers at the top: view, state, width ("Fit" follows the window), theme. URL: ?v=projects|agents|details|needs&s=<state>&w=fit|420|900|1280&t=light|dark.
  • Mock behaviour, clickable: task titles (Details), Stop (native dialog, Keep running focused), Resume, Undo merge (native dialog), Apply / Discard / Let Manta decide, Submit / Let it guess, Undo on the "done" rows, "Should have asked me" in Details, the Projects/Agents switch (remembered), Esc closes Details. Nothing talks to a box.
  • Loading shows nothing for 150 ms, then the skeleton.

Defaults applied (the nine decisions)

  • 1 Checklist bar kept; no "N of M" text, no project sums. 2 Let Manta decide takes the recommended option, with Undo. 3 Undo merge on Manta-merged Ended rows for 24 h. 4 Ended keeps today, "New" markers, "Earlier days". 5 Resume disabled with its reason when the box is full. 6 No Manta group; "asked by Manta in <chat>". 7 Usual range only past its upper bound and with 10+ runs. 8 A failed task goes to Ended and the asking chat; Needs you only when a decision is needed. 9 Sidebar Fleet count "5", "box full" in the header.
