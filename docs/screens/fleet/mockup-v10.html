Mockup notes (throwaway)

How to use it

  • Switchers at the top: view, state, width ("Fit" follows the window), theme. URL: ?v=projects|agents|details|needs&s=<state>&w=fit|420|900|1280&t=light|dark.
  • Mock behaviour, clickable: task titles (Details, where the technical detail lives), Stop (in Details, native dialog), Resume (on rows paused by you), Apply / Discard, Submit, Undo on the "done" rows in Needs you, "Should have asked me" in Details, the Projects/Agents switch (remembered), Esc closes Details. Nothing talks to a box.

v10 in one line

  • Fleet reads like a manager's overview: what's being worked on, whether it's going fine, and whether anything needs you. Everything technical is one click away in Details. Only blocking decisions show at the top; questions live in Needs you. Rows show only statuses the box records reliably: In progress, or Paused by you.
